## Scope and limitations

The three production classifiers follow the reviewed patch, preserving each ordinary
error branch and rethrowing the caught SomeException on asynchronous family membership.
The publisher queue is registered directly to force full fetch and bracket unsubscribe.
The existing synchronous publisher recovery example additionally verifies checkpoint
and publisher progress after retry.

The test-only notifier seam reads an optional IORef once at startup, inside the real
LISTEN catch and connection bracket. There is no changed event-append SQL, pool
checkout, checkpoint statement, per-event allocation, or callback-free emit path.
No remote benchmark is warranted by this exceptional-path fix and single startup read;
structural performance checks provide the proportional performance evidence.

Adjacent notifier handlers retain their concrete AsyncCancelled policy. Wait cancellation
exits normally; reacquisition cancellation is rethrown. Callback cancellation either
reaches that wait handler or escapes a recovery handler. stopNotifier still owns final
connection release. Other asynchronous types can still trigger reconnect in those later
handlers; this change makes no broader notifier-policy guarantee.

This evidence does not establish all shutdown interleavings, consumer SIGTERM deployment
symptoms, or the separate consumer OpenTelemetry issue resolved. Historical compatibility
patches and their evidence remain unchanged. See [ADR-23](../../../adr/0023-recovery-boundaries-preserve-asynchronous-exception-family-membership.md)
for the durable policy.
